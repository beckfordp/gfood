# Personas

Five people, one per side plus a second customer whose needs differ in ways that
matter (allergies, a bigger basket). Each ends with the stories the persona drives.

## Asha, 34 — customer, weeknight regular

> "Tell me when it'll be here, and tell me straight away if that changes."

Product manager, works late, orders two or three times a week, usually the same few
places. Orders on the phone on the way home.

- **Goals:** an ETA that holds; reorder a favourite in two taps; problems fixed without
  a phone call.
- **Frustrations:** ETAs that slip without a word; a missing side and a refund form;
  paying a fee she didn't see coming.
- **Drives:** ETA and progress, notifications, fee breakdown, reorder, report a problem.

## Dan, 41 — customer, Friday family order

> "My son has a nut allergy. If I can't trust the menu, I won't order."

Orders for four on Friday nights; a bigger basket with options for each child. Reads
every item's details.

- **Goals:** allergen information he can trust; options per item (no cheese, small
  size); food arriving together and hot.
- **Frustrations:** allergens missing or vague; an item silently swapped; a cold half of
  the order.
- **Drives:** modifiers, allergens, sold-out items shown before ordering, partial
  refunds.

## Rosa, 52 — restaurant owner, 30-seat pizzeria

> "Friday at eight I've got one hand on the oven. The tablet has to be simple or it
> gets ignored."

Runs a busy neighbourhood pizzeria; delivery is a fifth of revenue. The tablet sits at
the pass.

- **Goals:** never miss an order; pause new orders when the kitchen is slammed; mark the
  burrata sold out in one tap.
- **Frustrations:** couriers crowding the door too early; orders auto-cancelled while
  she's plating; commission she can't reconcile.
- **Drives:** accept with prep time, pause, sold out, timed dispatch, payout statement.

## Kofi, 27 — part-time e-bike courier

> "Every minute I stand at a counter is a minute I'm not paid for."

Studies by day, rides evenings and weekends. Chooses when to be online; compares
earnings per hour across apps.

- **Goals:** jobs close by, with the fee shown up front; food ready when he arrives;
  clear drop-off details.
- **Frustrations:** long waits at restaurants; wrong or vague addresses; not knowing
  what a job pays until after.
- **Drives:** online/offline, offers with fee, decline, waiting time paid, proof of
  delivery, earnings.

## Mei, 29 — customer-care agent

> "Show me the whole timeline and I can fix it in a minute."

Handles chats about late, missing and wrong orders, within refund rules the business
sets.

- **Goals:** one view of an order's full timeline; refund an item or the order in one
  action; spot repeat claimers.
- **Frustrations:** piecing events together from several screens; refund rules applied
  by hand.
- **Drives:** order lookup and timeline, refund or cancel, reassign
  courier.
